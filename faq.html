Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering and picking up. Still curious? Give us a call or drop by the counter.

When can I pick up my order?
We're open for pickup on Saturdays, 8:00 AM–1:00 PM, or until we sell out. Choose your Saturday when you place your order.
Do I need an account to order?
No accounts, no passwords. Just fill in the order form with your name, contact info, and items. We'll confirm by phone or email.
How far ahead do I need to order?
Please place pickup orders by Thursday evening for that Saturday. We bake to order in small batches, so earlier is safer for popular items. Walk-ins are welcome too, while supplies last.
How and when do I pay?
Payment is taken at pickup — no prepayment needed. We accept cash and major cards.
Do you have vegetarian or gluten-free options?
Most of our menu is vegetarian, and we mark items with V (vegetarian) and GF (gluten-free) on the menu. Our Flourless Chocolate Cookie is a reliable gluten-free favorite.
What about allergens?
Our kitchen handles wheat, eggs, dairy, and nuts, so we can't guarantee any item is fully free of cross-contact. If you have a serious allergy, note it on your order or call us and we'll help you choose.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Of course — just reply to your confirmation or call us before the Thursday cutoff and we'll sort it out.
Do you cater or take large orders?
We love a big order! For events or bulk quantities, reach out a week ahead at hello@example.com so we can plan the bake.
