Our Story

A one-day-a-week bakery built on slow ferments, simple ingredients, and a love of feeding the neighborhood.

Saturday Bakehouse started as a home kitchen experiment — one sourdough starter, a borrowed Dutch oven, and far too many loaves to eat alone. Word spread down the block, and Saturdays became baking days.

Today we still bake just once a week, on purpose. Baking a single day lets us do it properly: long overnight ferments, dough shaped by hand, and everything pulled from the oven that same morning. Nothing sits around. What we bake Saturday, we sell Saturday.
